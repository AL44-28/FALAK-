# Earth imagery

`earth.jpg` is the NASA Earth Observatory Blue Marble: Next Generation July 2004 global base map, 5400 × 2700 pixels. Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Reto Stöckli.

Source page: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
Original image: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg

This is static reference imagery. It does not show conditions at the simulated epoch. The viewer uses a bright illustrative daylight treatment for geographic readability, not a calculated Sun terminator. Orbital positions and coverage calculations do not depend on the texture.
